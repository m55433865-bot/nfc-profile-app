FAQ

Quick answers.

Works on iPhone and Android?

Yes. NFC works on most modern phones, and every card can also be shared with a QR code.

Does the other person need an app?

No. Your profile opens in the browser, so visitors can connect without installing anything.

Can I edit later?

Yes. Update your name, bio, links, theme, QR, and contact details whenever you want.

What happens if I lose the card?

Your profile stays online. You can keep using your QR link and replace the physical card when needed.

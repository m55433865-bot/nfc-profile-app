FAQ

Quick answers.

Works on iPhone and Android?

Yes. NFC works on most modern phones. The QR fallback is there for phones, booths, print, and situations where scanning is easier.

Does the other person need an app?

No. The profile opens in the browser, so the other person can view your details without installing anything.

Can I change my links later?

Yes. Update your phone, bio, socials, website, location, theme, and other links without printing a new card.

What happens if I lose the card?

Your profile stays online. You can keep using your profile link or QR while arranging a replacement card.

Can I use it for my business?

Yes. It works well for service businesses, sales teams, creators, real estate agents, coaches, salons, and freelancers.

What should I put on my profile?

Start with your phone, WhatsApp, Instagram or LinkedIn, location or booking link, and one clear action you want people to take.
